WHAT IT IS

A tabletop dogfight, rebuilt for the screen.

Squadron Holotable follows the rules of the X-Wing miniatures game (2.5 edition) to the millimetre: real maneuver templates, firing arcs, range bands, red and green dice, and a damage deck. Nothing is simplified, and the computer does all the measuring.

It is a game of reading your opponent, not of reflexes. Every ship commits to its move before anyone sees anything. Out-think the other pilot and they fly straight into your guns. Guess wrong and you are the one staring at empty space.

HOW A ROUND WORKS

Four beats, then you plan again.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Give every ship a maneuver: a speed and a direction. Both players plan at once, and nobody sees the other side's dials.

  2. 02

    Fly

    Dials flip and ships move one at a time, least skilled pilots first. Aces move last, so they get to react. After moving, a ship takes one action: focus, evade, lock a target, or barrel roll.

  3. 03

    Fight

    Now the best pilots shoot first. Red attack dice against green defense dice. Close range helps the shooter; long range and asteroids help the target. The tokens you took earlier fix bad rolls.

  4. 04

    Reset

    Tokens clear and you plan again. Destroying a ship scores its point cost. First to 20 points wins, or whoever leads after 12 rounds.

YOUR FIRST GAME

You can be flying in about a minute.

  1. STEP 1

    Launch against the AI

    Press Play now, stay on Versus AI, and keep the squads it suggests. Set the opponent to Rookie for a forgiving first fight.

  2. STEP 2

    Deploy

    Click inside the glowing strip on your edge of the table to place each ship. Keep them close enough to help each other.

  3. STEP 3

    Set your dials

    Click a ship and choose a maneuver from the dial. Farther from the centre is faster. Hover to see the ghost, then press Lock in maneuvers.

  4. STEP 4

    Take an action

    After each of your ships moves, the game offers its actions. Not sure? Take Focus. It helps on attack and on defense.

  5. STEP 5

    Shoot

    When an enemy is inside a ship's firing arc, pick the target. The dice roll in the open, and the game offers your Focus or Lock when they would help.

  6. STEP 6

    Do it again, smarter

    Every round you know a little more about how your opponent flies. Use it.

Reading the dial

Blue is easy flying. It clears one stress.

White is ordinary. No effect.

Red is a hard maneuver, like a flip turn. It causes stress, and a stressed ship cannot take actions or fly red again.

Three ways rookies die

Flying off the table. A ship that leaves the play area is gone.

Clipping an asteroid. It costs hull, and you cannot shoot while touching one.

Forgetting that most ships only shoot forward. Get behind them.

WAYS TO PLAY

Alone, side by side, or across the world.

Versus AI

Rookie, Veteran, or Ace, with three flying styles. The AI plans the way you do: it predicts where you will go and never peeks at your dials.

Hotseat

Two players, one screen. The game blanks the table between turns so you can plan in secret and hand over the controls.

Online

Create a room and send the five-letter code. No accounts. The server rolls every die and holds both sets of dials, so nobody can cheat.
